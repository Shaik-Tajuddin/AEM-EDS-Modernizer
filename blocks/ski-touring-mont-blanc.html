Ski Touring Mont Blanc

📍 Chamonix • Haute-Savoie • France

The Mont Blanc massif is the ultimate ski touring destination in Europe. With soaring granite spires and vast, heavily glaciated terrain, Chamonix is the spiritual home of steep skiing and grand high-mountain traverses.

On this five-day expedition, you will traverse iconic glaciers, conquer demanding ascents on skins, and descend legendary couloirs under the supervision of certified IFMGA mountain guides.

Expedition Highlights

⛷️ 2,000m vertical untracked descents
🏔️ Overnight in Argentière & Grand Mulets huts
🛡️ IFMGA certified lead mountain guides
❄️ Crevasse rescue & avalanche safety coaching

Day 1: Arrival & Safety Briefing in Chamonix

Meet guides at chalet. Gear checks, avalanche transceiver beacon drills, and weather/snowpack assessment.

Day 2: Grands Montets Warm-up & Glacier Skills

Cable car ascent to 3,275m. Shake-out tour on Glacier des Rognons and companion rescue refresher.

Day 3: Col du Tour Noir Skinning Ascent (3,535m)

Early alpine start from Argentière hut across Glacier des Améthystes with 4-hour skinning climb and powder run.

Day 4: Col du Passon & Le Tour Descent

Bootpacking couloir with crampons and ice axe, followed by an exhilarating 1,500m descent to Le Tour.

Day 5: Grands Mulets Ascent & Valley Celebration

High altitude ski touring overlooking the Bossons glacier before returning to Chamonix for celebratory dinner.

Technical alpine touring requires lightweight, breathable, and waterproof layering designed for extreme alpine weather:

Required Technical Gear

  • Touring skis with pin/tech bindings, fitted climbing skins, and ski crampons (harscheisen)
  • Touring boots with walk-mode articulation and Vibram soles
  • Digital 3-antenna avalanche transceiver, 240cm+ avalanche probe, and metal blade shovel
  • Lightweight alpine harness, 2 locking carabiners, and 45cm steel ice axe
  • 10 or 12-point boot crampons fitted to your touring boots

Apparel & Layering

  • 3-Layer Gore-Tex waterproof breathable shell jacket and bib pants
  • Packable 800-fill down or Primaloft active insulation hoody
  • Merino wool thermal base layers (top and bottom)
  • Two pairs of gloves (windproof touring gloves + insulated waterproof mittens)
  • Cat 3/4 glacier sunglasses and anti-fog low-light ski goggles
