Overview

The Mont Blanc massif is the ultimate ski touring destination in Europe. With soaring granite spires and vast, heavily glaciated terrain, Chamonix is the spiritual home of steep skiing and grand high-mountain traverses.

On this five-day expedition, you will traverse iconic glaciers, conquer demanding ascents on skins, and descend legendary couloirs under the supervision of certified IFMGA mountain guides.

Highlights

  • High alpine ski touring through the heart of the Mont Blanc range
  • Overnight stays in historic mountain huts (Argentière and Grand Mulets)
  • Comprehensive avalanche safety and crevasse rescue coaching
  • Unrivaled 2,000m vertical powder descents to the valley floor
Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Chamonix

Meet our lead mountain guides at the chalet in Chamonix in the afternoon. Comprehensive briefing on weather forecasts, snowpack stability, route logistics, avalanche transceiver testing, and mandatory gear checks.

Day 2: Grands Montets Warm-up & Avalanche Rescue Drill

Ascent via Grands Montets cable car to test touring gear on steep glaciated terrain. Afternoon workshop on companion rescue, beacon searches, probe technique, and snow pit analysis.

Day 3: Col du Tour Noir Traverse

Depart from the Argentière hut across Glacier des Améthystes toward Col du Tour Noir (3,535m). Spectacular 4-hour skinning ascent followed by an exhilarating glacier descent.

Day 4: Col du Passon & Descent to Le Tour

Early alpine start over the Col du Passon with crampons and ice axes over the summit couloir. A 1,500m untracked descent into the scenic village of Le Tour.

Day 5: Grand Mulets Ascent & Valley Return

Aiguille du Midi cable car to the Grand Mulets refuge route. High altitude touring with panoramic vistas across the Bossons glacier before returning to Chamonix for celebratory dinner.

What to Bring

When selecting clothing and technical gear for Mont Blanc ski touring, prioritize lightweight, breathable, and weather-proof layers:

Technical Gear

  • All-mountain touring skis with pin/tech bindings and fitted skins
  • Ski touring boots with walk-mode articulation
  • 3-antenna digital avalanche transceiver, 240cm+ probe, and metal shovel
  • Lightweight alpine harness, 2 locking carabiners, and ice axe
  • Boot crampons compatible with your touring boots

Apparel & Layering

  • 3-Layer Gore-Tex waterproof breathable shell jacket and pants
  • Packable 800-fill down or Primaloft insulation jacket
  • Merino wool base layers (top and bottoms)
  • Two pairs of gloves (lightweight touring gloves + waterproof alpine mittens)
  • Cat 3/4 glacier sunglasses and anti-fog ski goggles
